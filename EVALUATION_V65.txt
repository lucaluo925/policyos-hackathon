Claim: Businesses established in France and subject to VAT must be able to
receive electronic invoices from 1 September 2026, while SMEs and
micro-enterprises have until 1 September 2027 to issue them.
Expected / observed: supported / supported.
Pipeline time: 18.814 seconds. Tavily calls: 5. Model calls: 5.
Review persistence: 2 assertions saved. Stage errors: 0.

dates but omits the VAT scope. The second assertion's tax-authority excerpt
